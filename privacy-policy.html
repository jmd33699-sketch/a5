1. Fundamental Principles of Minimal Data Ingestion

At LightweightParka, our approach to digital privacy mirrors our philosophy in outerwear engineering: ruthless elimination of non-essential weight. We do not operate advertising trackers, we do not participate in third-party data broker syndicates, and we never sell user telemetry to marketing aggregators.

2. Categories of Information Collected

We restrict data collection strictly to technical interactions necessary to maintain site security, process customer inquiries, and evaluate aggregate performance:

  • Direct Communications Data: Information provided explicitly when submitting an inquiry form, including your name, email address, and expedition requirements.
  • Pseudonymous Analytics Telemetry: Aggregated pageview data processed via Google Analytics (GA4 tag G-0LY0HY7L01) with IP anonymization enabled by default.
  • Essential Session State: Local browser storage used solely to persist user UI preferences such as packability calculator inputs and search indices.

3. International Data Transfers & Storage Security

All data transmitted through lightweightparka.com is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with modern forward secrecy ciphers. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standard encryption on audited, SOC2 Type II compliant server infrastructure.

4. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Regardless of geographic residency, all visitors are granted the comprehensive right to inspect, correct, export, or permanently delete any personal data associated with their interaction. To initiate a data rights request, transmit your query directly to our Data Protection Officer at contact@lightweightparka.com.
