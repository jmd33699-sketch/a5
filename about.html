Our Heritage

Minimalist Engineering For Maximum Exposure

Born out of the Scottish Highlands and the Chamonix Valley, LightweightParka was founded to liberate alpine athletes from the burden of heavy, cumbersome foul-weather gear.

The Weight-to-Protection Paradox

For decades, outdoor gear manufacturers insisted that genuine storm protection required heavy, multi-layer Gore-Tex fabrics weighing over half a kilogram. Mountaineers faced an agonizing compromise: either carry exhausting dead weight in their packs, or gamble with flimsy windshells that wetting-out inside forty minutes of sustained rainfall.

LightweightParka was established in 2021 by a consortium of alpine guides, textile polymer chemists, and aerospace composites engineers. Our founding mission was unyielding: synthesize an impermeable, 3-layer storm shell that weighed under 135 grams while surpassing JIS L 1092-B waterproof standards.

By reimagining every element—from single-filament 7-denier high-tenacity yarns to 8mm ultrasonic hot melt tapes—we eliminated over 300 grams of dead weight without sacrificing a single millimeter of hydrostatic barrier defense.

Zero Compromise

Empirical Laboratory Rigor

Every textile bolt undergoes 500 hours of continuous hydro-head testing, flexing cycles at -25°C, and Martindale abrasion resistance testing before it enters production cutting.
